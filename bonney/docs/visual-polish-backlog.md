# Visual polish backlog - proposed
Companion: visual-audit-2026-11-02.md.
This backlog is a proposal, not implemented work.

## Pass A: hierarchy and framing
- Reduce ordinary coin display to 8-10 world pixels while retaining generous collection bounds.
- Replace uniform coin generation with hand-authored arcs/clusters and quiet sections.
- Prototype approximately 80-100 total coins; tune after viewing/playtesting.
- Remove roughly half the regularly repeated prop groups as a starting edit.
- Preserve enemy silhouettes, warning cues and landing zones.
- Group required outfits into clear supply stations without losing safe access.
- Prototype a 26-30px HUD, transient help text and lower grounded camera framing.
Exit gate: an uncluttered route with a clear player/threat/landing hierarchy.

## Pass B: finished Market Quay slice
- Recompose 2-3 screens, including a calm view, a taught encounter and a reward/branch.
- Create one market frontage/awning kit in the established palette.
- Quiet the far scenery using art-specific treatment without rectangular overlays.
- Establish layered parallax and collision vs non-collision visual language.
- Review baby/teen/adult and both outfits in motion.
Exit gate: this slice becomes the approved quality standard before the whole route is repainted.

## Pass C: district identity
- Warehouse facade/roof/catwalk kit.
- Crane gantry/cables/pulley assembly.
- Customs architecture and bounded gate aperture.
- Shipyard hull/slipway silhouettes.
- Flagship deck/cabin/rigging kit.
- Reconnect puzzle mechanisms visibly to gate/bridge actions.
Exit gate: district recognizable without the text label.

## Pass D: encounter and secret composition
- Hand-place every enemy and routine reward pattern.
- Introduce, combine and rest encounters deliberately.
- Move rare treasures above common currency in visual importance.
- Add visible secret clues and safe return routes.
- Preserve puzzle recovery and age-invariant movement.
Exit gate: no accidental cue overlap, required damage, blind hazard or outfit soft lock.

## Pass E: final polish and QA
- Consistent animation anticipation / impact / recovery.
- Shared lighting direction, palette, outline and material rules.
- Native-size and grayscale review, motion capture, boundary sweeps.
- Frame-time measurement and culling review.
- Blind player navigation/difficulty tests.
Exit gate: documented art/readability checks and gameplay regression tests pass together.

Progress update: the first art-direction pass now covers all six districts. See market-polish-v1.md, warehouse-polish-v1.md and remaining-district-polish-v1.md. Reward/decor placement, architecture landmarks, HUD and camera hierarchy are implemented. Further gate architecture/collision refinement, animation polish, performance profiling and blind human playtests remain review items.

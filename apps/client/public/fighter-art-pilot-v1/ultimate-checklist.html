Art production gates

  1. Choose each original fighter's attack gestures, specials and optional abilities; keep Universal Ultimate behavior separate from fighter-specific kits.
  2. Draw the kicks, dash attack, smash charge/release phases, aerials, specials and grab/victim pairs. Complete movement, defense, floor recovery and ledge transitions from the full inventory.
  3. Register roots, handedness, body scale and hand/foot/ledge/item sockets. Preserve the accepted walk cadence and existing pilot images.
  4. Record startup, active/contact, hold, release, recovery and interrupt markers. Exact simulation durations come from verified move data later, not from fixed raster frame counts.
  5. Approve all three packs, four-player material variants and companion effects. Then implement and test engine parity, including the existing timing disagreements.

Conditional content is not removed from scope. Items, water, ladders, Final Smashes and kit abilities require their content/design choices before their bundled rows can expand into complete per-item/per-ability sheets. Exact Ultimate patch values remain to be verified.

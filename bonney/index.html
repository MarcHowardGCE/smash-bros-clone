Egghead

Jump, then press Space again to use up to 5 seconds of flight fuel. Hold Space to rise; release to fall under gravity. Only lift uses fuel. Landing starts the 5-second cooldown.

Pirate Captain

Press X to charge as a cannonball. A new power-up replaces your current one and is ready immediately. Full recharge takes 5 seconds. Use a partially filled bar for a shorter ability.

Three chances

Adult → teenage → baby → out. Stomp enemies or charge through them. A fall costs one hit. In Harbor Breakout, defeat lets you retry from the checkpoint as adult; treasures and switches stay secured.

Enemy field guide

Patrol: stomp or cannonball. Lookout: watch the aim cue, dodge the shot, approach during recovery. Shield: bait its swing, then attack from behind or above. Gull: its warning marks a committed dive; move off that line.

E operates purple switches while Egghead is equipped. Food restores one age stage. R / Restart resets the whole run; Continue from checkpoint preserves treasures and secured mechanisms after defeat.

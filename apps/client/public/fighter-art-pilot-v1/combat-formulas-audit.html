Combat formula audit and corrections

4 November 2026 · batch 32

Weighted stale moves, launch percent, hitstun and DI have been corrected. Rage was verified. The human-approved contact geometry is retained.

Play updated local build · Evidence and limits · 54 independent comparison cases

Stale moves

Nine-entry history now uses recency weights: 9%, 8.545%, 7.635%, 6.79%, 5.945%, 5.035%, 4.255%, 3.345%, 2.5% from newest to oldest. Unused moves keep the 1.05 freshness bonus. Nine repeats produce a 0.4695 damage multiplier.

Shield history and throws

Shield contacts contribute 85% of their stale penalty. A parallel shield/body history is replicated and kept aligned as entries expire. Throws now enter history alongside ordinary attacks and pummels.

Knockback

Normal hit resolution includes the current hit in launch percent. The launch base uses the reference 30% stale adjustment, separate from damage scaling. Existing one-on-one percent damage remains excluded from the current hit’s launch calculation.

Rage

Verified the existing curve: no increase through 35%, rising to 1.1 at 150%, then capped. Boundary tests cover the unchanged curve.

Hitstun

Updated to floor(knockback × 0.4) − 1, with a four-tick minimum. The previous three-tick subtraction was a project approximation.

Directional influence

Maximum rotation is now 0.17 radians. Digital diagonal input is normalized; horizontal launches use a DI deadzone. This is the project’s digital-input adaptation, not full analog-stick certification.

Research reference

Calculator author’s formulas.js. Download SHA256: 882fd5cf49c9cfecad39ac1807ab827350ccf9dbd62adaad8a4a0bf16a1e7133. The 54 generated cases span three fighter weights, three starting percentages, fresh/singly stale/fully stale moves, two damage values and mid-curve rage. Expected values were generated independently of the game implementation.

Matching this research implementation does not certify a complete or version-specific Ultimate ruleset. Digital input and world-space motion still have explicit limits below.

Validation

All 1,320 workspace tests passed (17 skipped), including 54 independently generated formula cases. Type checks passed. The final throw-history assertions passed in the 11-case grab suite.

All 48 review/local browser cases and the separate four-client test passed (49 total). Staged production loaded all fourteen live atlas pages with no failed requests or page errors.

A fresh 96-match hard-CPU stability run had 71 finishes and 25 timeouts; all tracked state remained finite. Timed-out matches have no winner. These are stability checks, not human balance certification.

Remaining work

Hitlag

Move-authored hitlag ticks remain. The reference uses damage, attack attributes and participant count. Add explicit hitlag multipliers and projectile/electric metadata before replacing authored timings.

SDI / ASDI

Current held-input SDI repeats on a four-tick cooldown, and ASDI drifts throughout hitstun up to a cap. These remain project rules; verify flick detection, displacement in world units and hitlag-exit-only behavior.

Launch physics / LSI

Formula magnitude is compared with the research reference, but world-space launch speed, decay, gravity interactions and launch-speed influence remain project calibration.

Special cases

Counters retain their custom damage/launch rules. Clanks, multi-target move handling, automatic angles, shields, techs and ledge timing still need versioned validation.

Human and remote testing

Accepted hit areas are retained. Re-test repeated attacks, fresh finishers, DI survival and combo escape; verify physical pads, latency/loss and target-device performance.

Egghead

Jump, then press Space again to use up to 5 seconds of flight fuel. Hold Space to rise; release to fall under gravity. Only lift uses fuel. Landing starts the 5-second cooldown.

Pirate Captain

Press X to charge as a cannonball. A new power-up replaces your current one and is ready immediately. Marked supply trays let you choose with E (controller Y). Full recharge takes 5 seconds. Use a partially filled bar for a shorter ability.

Gear form

Move 1.5× faster. Press Space again in the air for a spinning double jump. Tap Z to punch; hold Z until charged and release for a shield-breaking lunge. C kicks. The special recharges in 5 seconds; partial energy gives a shorter lunge.

Power protection

An enemy hit removes your equipped power first. Further hits: adult → teenage → baby → out. Powered adult has four hits total. Punch, kick, stomp enemies, or use a special. Falls follow the current area’s rules. In Harbor Breakout, defeat lets you retry from the checkpoint as adult; treasures and switches stay secured.

Sky robots: walkers patrol the clouds, hover drones telegraph shots, and sentinels guard their fronts. Charged Gear punches break shields. Thin clouds fade underfoot; fan currents lift you upward. Secure the reactor controls to reach the cyan flag. Falling returns you to the harbor; cloud checkpoints are retained.

Diving suit: powers are stowed until you surface. Walk and run more slowly; hold Space for high grounded jumps, or tap for a low hop. No swimming or flight. Z punches, C kicks, E turns valves and powers relays. Vents warn before releasing pressure. Bottomless falls cost one age; harbor water entry does not.

Enemy field guide

Patrol: stomp or cannonball. Lookout: watch the aim cue, dodge the shot, approach during recovery. Shield: bait its swing, then attack from behind or above. Gull: its warning marks a committed dive; move off that line.

E operates marked controls; their nearby prompt identifies any required outfit. Food restores one age stage. R / Restart resets the whole run; Continue from checkpoint preserves treasures and secured mechanisms after defeat.
